## Services we provide in Lake Mathews

Water damage in Lake Mathews often starts below the house rather than above it. Well pump failures, slab leaks under older copper lines, and septic backups push moisture into crawlspaces and subfloor framing, where it can sit undetected until flooring starts to cup or a musty smell shows up in a back bedroom. We extract standing water, pull moisture readings from framing and insulation, and set containment drying equipment sized to the space rather than guessing.

Fire and smoke damage here frequently comes from wildfire exposure rather than a structure fire itself. Smoke and ash can work into HVAC systems, insulation, and soft materials even when the flames never reached the property line, and we clean and deodorize accordingly, following EPA guidance on smoke residue removal.

Mold remediation matters in Lake Mathews because of how long moisture can go unnoticed in a crawlspace or outbuilding before anyone catches it. Mold can begin colonizing damp wood and drywall within 24 to 48 hours of a water intrusion, which is why we treat any standing water call as a potential mold job from the start, not an afterthought.

Biohazard and trauma cleanup, along with sewage backup cleanup tied to septic failures, round out what we handle locally, always under OSHA bloodborne pathogen protocols where applicable.
