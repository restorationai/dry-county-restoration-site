## Why Santa Ana Properties See Leak Detection Issues

A large share of homes in Santa Ana were built during the postwar tract boom, with copper plumbing routed through concrete slab foundations, a standard method for that era across Orange County. Copper has a long service life, but after several decades it develops pinhole leaks from pitting corrosion, and because the pipe is embedded in or under the slab, the first sign is usually a hot spot on the floor, a hissing sound near a wall, or a water meter that keeps spinning even with every fixture off. The region's expansive clay soils also shift seasonally, and that movement puts stress on rigid pipe joints that were never designed to flex. Add in the dry climate, where there's little ambient moisture to make a leak obvious, and problems that would show up fast elsewhere can run for weeks here before anyone notices.

## Our Water Leak Detection Process in Santa Ana

We start by ruling out the obvious, checking irrigation lines, water heater connections, and visible plumbing before assuming a slab or in-wall leak. From there we use acoustic listening equipment to pick up the sound of pressurized water escaping through a pipe, which carries differently through slab concrete than through open-frame walls. Thermal imaging helps confirm a suspected location by reading temperature differences at the surface, since an active leak under a slab often reads several degrees warmer or cooler than the surrounding floor. Where needed, we pair that with moisture meters to map how far water has traveled, because a leak's source point and its visible damage are frequently several feet apart once water has tracked along a slab or stud bay. The goal is always to pinpoint the leak with minimal exploratory demolition, since every unnecessary cut adds to the repair bill.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Leak Detection

Slab leaks and in-wall leaks call for different tools, and we bring both. Acoustic correlators help isolate a leak's position along a pipe run by comparing sound arrival times at two points, which works well on the rigid copper and older galvanized lines still common in Santa Ana's established neighborhoods. For wall cavities, a combination of moisture mapping and borescope inspection lets us look inside a wall through a small access point rather than opening an entire section of drywall. When a slab leak is suspected, we also check static water pressure against the meter to confirm there's an active leak before any demolition starts, since a false positive means cutting into a floor for nothing.

## Santa Ana Insurance & HOA Coordination

Many Santa Ana condos and attached homes fall under an HOA, and most associations require advance notice before any flooring or slab work begins, along with documentation of the cause before approving repairs on shared plumbing lines.
