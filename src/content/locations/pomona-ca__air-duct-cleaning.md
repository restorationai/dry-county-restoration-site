Pomona's inland valley location puts it squarely in the path of Santa Ana wind events and long stretches of dry summer heat, and that combination pushes an unusual amount of fine dust, pollen, and wildfire ash into home HVAC systems each year. If your supply registers leave a gray film on furniture within weeks of dusting, or your system seems to run longer than it should to reach the same temperature, the ductwork itself is often the reason. Air duct cleaning here isn't cosmetic, it's a response to a climate that genuinely loads systems faster than coastal cities see.

## Why Pomona Properties See Duct Issues

Much of Pomona's residential stock dates to the postwar boom through the 1970s, with sheet metal duct runs that have been patched, rerouted, or tied into newer HVAC equipment over decades of ownership changes. Flex duct added during later remodels often sags at the joints, creating low spots where dust and debris settle instead of moving through the system. Add the valley's seasonal Santa Ana wind pattern, which drives particulate matter into attics and crawlspaces through vents and soffits, and you get ducts that accumulate buildup noticeably faster than in milder, wetter parts of Southern California. Homes near busier corridors through the San Gabriel Valley also pull in more roadway dust through return air intakes, which settles inside the duct runs long before anyone notices a smell or a dust ring.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Pomona

We start with a camera inspection of the supply and return runs to see what's actually inside before any equipment goes to work, since a system choked with rodent activity in an older attic run needs a different approach than one with ordinary seasonal dust load. From there we use negative-air vacuum equipment connected directly to the duct system so loosened debris is pulled out rather than pushed further into the home, combined with agitation tools sized to the duct diameter. Register covers, supply boots, and the return plenum all get cleaned individually, and for homes with gas furnaces we check the heat exchanger and blower compartment while we're already inside the cabinet. Dryer vent cleaning is handled as a separate line item when requested, since lint-packed dryer vents are a distinct fire risk from general HVAC duct buildup and get their own clearance check at the exterior termination point.

## Local note

A detail that catches a lot of homeowners off guard: Pomona's older homes with original metal ductwork often have asbestos-containing duct wrap or mastic sealant on joints installed before the 1980s. We don't disturb that material during a standard cleaning without first identifying it, because agitating it the wrong way creates a bigger problem than the dust buildup we were called to fix. If a duct run looks original to the house and the insulation wrap looks aged or crumbling, we flag it before starting work rather than after.
