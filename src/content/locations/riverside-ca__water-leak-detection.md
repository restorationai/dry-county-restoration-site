**Hidden water leak in your Riverside home?** We answer 24/7 and bring the diagnostic equipment needed to find a slab leak or a leak inside a wall without tearing the property apart. Riverside's clay-heavy soil and decades-old copper plumbing make hidden leaks a common call for us, and the longer a leak runs under a slab or behind drywall, the more expensive the repair and the drying bill both become.

## Why Riverside Properties See Leak Detection Issues

Much of Riverside sits on expansive clay soils that swell and contract with the seasons, a pattern common across the Inland Empire. That movement stresses copper supply lines running under slab foundations, especially in homes built from the 1950s through the 1970s when copper was the standard. Add Riverside's long, hot dry season, which causes soil to shrink and shift even more as it dries out, and you get pipe joints working loose or pinholing years before a homeowner notices a water bill spike. Properties on or near older irrigation lines left over from the region's citrus-growing past sometimes have additional buried piping that was never mapped, which can complicate locating the actual source of a leak.

## Our Water Leak Detection Process in Riverside

We start with a water bill and pressure review, since a jump in usage with no visible change in habits is often the first real clue. From there we use acoustic listening equipment to trace the sound of water moving through pipe or escaping under pressure, paired with infrared thermal imaging to spot temperature differences in slab and wall surfaces where water is present. On slab-on-grade homes, common throughout Riverside's mid-century neighborhoods, we isolate sections of the plumbing system and pressure-test them individually rather than guessing at a single point of failure. Once we've confirmed the location, we mark it precisely so a plumber can make a targeted access cut instead of opening up an entire wall or slab section.

## Riverside Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most homeowner policies treat the cost of locating a hidden leak differently than the cost of repairing the plumbing itself, and carriers often want documented proof of the source before they'll approve coverage for resulting water damage. We photograph and document everything we find, including moisture readings and thermal images, so you have a clear record to hand to your adjuster. For condos and townhomes in Riverside's HOA-managed complexes, we coordinate with property management on access and shared-wall considerations before any invasive work begins, since leak paths in attached housing often cross unit lines.

## Local note

Riverside Public Utilities customers can sometimes catch a hidden leak early just by checking their RPU meter: if the small leak indicator dial is spinning with every fixture in the house off, water is moving somewhere it shouldn't be. We still recommend a professional locate before any cutting, though, because clay soil can mask the sound of a slab leak traveling several feet from where the water actually surfaces.
