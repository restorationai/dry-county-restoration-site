## Why Modjeska Properties See Water Damage Issues

Modjeska Canyon sits in the foothills along Santiago Canyon, and that geography shapes how water problems show up here. Many homes in the canyon rely on private wells and septic systems rather than municipal sewer, so a slab leak or failed water heater does not just flood a bathroom, it can push groundwater levels around a septic field or contaminate a well casing if the water sits long enough. Older cabin-style and ranch homes built into the hillside often sit on raised pier-and-beam or crawlspace foundations, which trap moisture against floor joists in ways a typical slab-on-grade tract house never does. After a wet winter or a wildfire season that strips vegetation from the surrounding canyon walls, runoff behaves differently too, water that used to soak into brush-covered slopes now sheets downhill and finds the low points around foundations and retaining walls.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Modjeska

We start with extraction, using truck-mount and portable pumps depending on how far equipment has to travel up a driveway or canyon access road. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed to account for the home's actual framing, whether that is open-web crawlspace venting or a tighter, older build with minimal subfloor airflow.
