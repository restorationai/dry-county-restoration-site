**Sewage backup in Long Beach?** Raw sewage in a bathroom, kitchen, or crawlspace is a biohazard the moment it hits the floor, and the clock on contamination spreading into subfloor and drywall starts immediately. ## Why Long Beach Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Long Beach sits on flat, low-lying coastal terrain with a water table that runs high close to the shoreline, and that combination puts real pressure on aging sewer laterals during heavy rain events or king tides. A large share of the city's housing stock dates to the early and mid-20th century, when clay and cast-iron sewer lines were standard. Those materials crack, root-intrude, and settle over decades, and a cracked lateral under a driveway or foundation slab is exactly the kind of failure that shows up as a slow drain one week and a full backup into a tub or floor drain the next. Combined sewer capacity strain during a storm surge can push flow backward into low-lying fixtures before a homeowner ever sees standing water outside.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process

We start by identifying the contamination category, since a sewer lateral backup is treated as Category 3 water: grossly contaminated and unsafe to touch without PPE. Crews extract standing waste first, then remove and bag porous materials that can't be sanitized, carpet pad, baseboard, drywall below the flood line. Remaining structural materials get cleaned, antimicrobially treated, and dried per the IICRC S500 standard, with containment barriers set up to keep aerosolized contaminants out of unaffected rooms. We document everything with photos before demolition begins, which matters when a claim depends on proving the extent of contamination rather than just the source.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Sewage Cleanup

Sewage jobs call for different equipment than a clean-water flood. We use truck-mount extraction for high-volume removal, HEPA air scrubbers to manage airborne contaminants during demolition, and EPA-registered antimicrobial treatments on salvageable structural surfaces. Moisture mapping with penetrating meters tells us how far contamination traveled under flooring and behind baseboards, since sewage water wicks laterally through subfloor in ways that aren't always visible from the surface. Negative air pressure containment keeps odor and pathogens from migrating through HVAC returns while work is underway.
